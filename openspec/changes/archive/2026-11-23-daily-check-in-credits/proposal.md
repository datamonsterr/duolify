# Daily check-in Credits

## Why
The owner (2026-11-22) chose automatic attendance grants to fund a new Learner's Course preview, Course outline and first Lesson. The 46 Daily free credits remain unchanged.

## What Changes
- Grant 150 check-in Credits per attended local day plus 250 once on the first check-in (400 on day one).
- Bank this separate, non-expiring source up to seven daily grants; spend it after eligible Daily free credits and before paid sources.
- Add an explicit authenticated check-in endpoint, accessible non-blocking confirmation, transactional analytics, privacy and backup coverage.

## Impact
Credits API, immutable Pricing config, wallet, account privacy, analytics catalogue and backup classification. No Engine, model, billing, Streak or full-stack flow changes. No new model behavior or Golden set cases are needed.
