## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Owner-approved preview and transcription pricing
An approved pricing version SHALL set preview output to 6,000 tokens without changing its Credit price and SHALL set transcription to zero Credits, a provider cost ceiling of USD 0.0005 per request and a daily count limit of 60 per Learner.

#### Scenario: Version is activated
- **WHEN** the approved successor version becomes active
- **THEN** unrelated prices and terms remain unchanged
