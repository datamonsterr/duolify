## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Copy budget per screen type
Every Learner-facing screen SHALL meet the copy budget for its type, measured on the English catalogue (Arabic uses the same word limits and character limits multiplied by 1.3). Budgets: heading at most 4 words and 24 characters; button, tab and chip label at most 2 words and 16 characters; caption or hint at most 1 line of 60 characters and at most 1 per card or section; toast at most 8 words and 48 characters; coach mark at most 10 words and 60 characters in one sentence; error message at most 12 words plus exactly one recovery action; system-authored chat turn at most 90 characters; sheet or dialog at most 8 distinct visible strings; any other screen at most 12 distinct visible strings, counting every visible text node except content marked `data-user-content` (Learner, course, lesson or AI generated text). Helper paragraphs, "how this works" blocks and instructions that an icon, position or control state already shows MUST NOT appear. Settings rows have a label of at most 3 words and no description line. Exemptions, each listed by message id in the audit: legal, consent and privacy disclosures; Credit costs, prices, plan periods and local-day resets (which stay explicit under "Localization-ready copy"); destructive-action confirmations; and accessible names that are not visible.

#### Scenario: Screen within budget
- **WHEN** the budget test opens the Courses screen at 390 by 844 in English
- **THEN** it has at most 12 distinct visible strings, every heading is at most 4 words, and no paragraph longer than 60 characters is visible

#### Scenario: Over budget fails the build
- **WHEN** a change adds a 90-character hint under a button
- **THEN** `mise run client:lint` fails naming the message id, its role and the limit it exceeds

#### Scenario: Explicit cost is exempt
- **WHEN** a button must show "Ask · 5 credits"
- **THEN** the cost is kept because it is a listed exemption, while the rest of the label stays within 2 words

### Requirement: Plain words
Learner-facing English SHALL be written at about grade 6: short common words, active voice, present tense, one idea per string, the verb first on actions ("Save note", not "Your note will be saved"). A string of 5 or more words MUST score a Flesch-Kincaid grade of 7 or lower, computed offline by the lint, and MUST NOT contain a word on the repository's plain-words denylist (for example "utilize", "authenticate", "configure", "enroll" where "join" works, "functionality"). Glossary terms from `CONTEXT.md` keep their meaning and spelling. Arabic and `en-XA` catalogues MUST keep the same keys and be reviewed for the same length and plainness rules by a native-speaking reviewer before release.

#### Scenario: Hard word
- **WHEN** a catalogue string contains "authenticate"
- **THEN** the lint fails and suggests the plain alternative from the denylist

#### Scenario: Arabic parity
- **WHEN** an English key is shortened or added
- **THEN** the Arabic catalogue has the same key, and the budget test passes in `ar` with `dir="rtl"` without clipped or overlapping text at 200% text size

### Requirement: Copy lives in messages catalogues
Every user-visible string, `aria-label`, `title`, `placeholder` and `alt` text in `apps/client/src` SHALL come from a `messages.ts` catalogue by stable id. A lint step run by `mise run client:lint` MUST parse the TypeScript sources, fail on a literal user-visible string outside a catalogue, and fail when a catalogue id is missing in `ar` or `en-XA`. Existing literals are recorded in a baseline file with one count per source file; the baseline MAY only go down, and every task in the per-screen passes removes the baseline entry for its screen.

#### Scenario: New literal
- **WHEN** a pull request adds `<button>Retry now</button>` to a component
- **THEN** the lint fails with the file, line and the instruction to add a catalogue id

#### Scenario: Baseline ratchet
- **WHEN** a pull request raises a file's baseline count
- **THEN** the lint fails; lowering the count passes

### Requirement: Copy audit
The change SHALL keep `docs/design/copy-audit.md`, generated by `mise run client:copy-audit`, listing every screen and sheet with its current and target string count, longest string, and failing ids, derived from the `messages.ts` catalogues and, until a screen migrates, from the baseline literals. Initial counts: the shared catalogue (`features/design/localization/messages.ts`) holds about 383 English strings (largest groups: tutor 83, la 62, outline 35, voice 35, demo 35, chat 19, shell 18, settings 16) and the onboarding catalogue about 37; roughly 500 more visible strings are inline literals in accounts, sharing, social, courses, credits, gamification, pricing, steering, characters, notes and lesson-player. Targets: every screen passes the budget; the total visible strings in the guided flow (onboarding, first course, first lesson) drop by at least 40%.

#### Scenario: Audit regenerated
- **WHEN** `mise run client:copy-audit` runs after a screen pass
- **THEN** that screen's current count equals its target and the audit file diff is committed with the pull request

### Requirement: Transient explanations instead of paragraphs
Explanations that remain necessary SHALL appear as one of three transient forms, never as a persistent paragraph: a toast (what just happened or why an action is unavailable), a first-use coach mark (how to use a control or gesture, shown once), or a long-press and hover tooltip (what an icon does). Toasts MUST use the `status` live region (errors `alert`), appear within 100 ms of the cause, stay 4 seconds (6 for errors, or until the Undo window ends), be dismissible by swipe toward the nearest edge and by Esc, sit above the bottom tab bar and safe area on phones and bottom-start on desktop, stack at most 2, and never carry the only path to a recovery. An undo toast MUST restore the prior state when its action is activated within 6 seconds. A coach mark MUST be anchored to its target, dismiss on any tap, on Esc or after use of the target, be shown at most once per Learner per id (stored in `gesture_hints_seen` or `hotkey_hints_seen`), at most one per screen visit, never on the first screen of onboarding, and never over a running lesson Slide. A tooltip MUST open on long-press (500 ms) or hover/focus (400 ms), contain at most 3 words plus the shortcut where one exists, and close on release, blur or Esc. All three MUST honour reduced motion and be readable at 200% text size.

#### Scenario: Unavailable action
- **WHEN** the Learner taps the disabled ask control while offline
- **THEN** a toast of at most 8 words says it needs a connection, and no paragraph is added to the screen

#### Scenario: Coach mark once
- **WHEN** a Learner opens a lesson on a phone for the first time
- **THEN** one coach mark of at most 10 words points at the stage swipe, a tap dismisses it, and it never appears again for that Learner on any device

#### Scenario: Undo
- **WHEN** a Learner deletes a note and activates Undo in the toast after 3 seconds
- **THEN** the note is restored with its anchor, and no deletion request is committed

#### Scenario: Reduced motion
- **WHEN** the system requests reduced motion
- **THEN** toasts and coach marks fade or appear without sliding or bouncing

### Requirement: Human-factors rules
Visual design SHALL follow these named principles, each reviewed by the design-review checklist and, where marked, enforced by an automated test.
- Fitts's law: the primary action of a phone view is bottom-anchored inside the thumb zone (lower 40% of the viewport, inline-end biased for one-handed use); every target is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels (48 on Android) with at least 8 px between neighbours; closing and back controls sit on the inline-start of a top bar and are mirrored by a swipe. Enforced by a bounding-box test at 390 by 844.
- Hick's law: a view has at most 1 primary and 2 secondary actions; everything else is behind one "more" control or a bottom sheet; menus show at most 7 items; choice lists longer than 7 are searchable or grouped. Enforced by counting `data-action-level` attributes.
- Recognition over recall: controls are icon plus label. Icon-only is allowed only for the universal set: play, pause, previous, next, close, back, more (three dots), search, add, send, microphone, volume and mute, full screen enter and exit, captions (CC), settings (gear), share, bookmark, home. Every other icon MUST carry a visible label of at most 2 words. Every icon-only control has an accessible name and a tooltip. The same function uses the same icon and the same position on every screen.
- Gestalt proximity and similarity: related controls are grouped within 8 px and separated from other groups by at least 24 px or a divider; controls of the same level share shape, size and colour; one card is one tap target for its main action.
- Colour semantics from existing tokens: `primary` fill marks the single primary action of a view; `success` with a check icon marks done and progress; `warning` and `streak` amber mark attention and expiring things; `danger` red marks destructive actions and errors only; `secondary` and `info` blue mark neutral help and secondary actions; `credit` purple marks Credits only; `accent` yellow is reserved for rewards. No new colour is introduced.
- Never colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1): state is always colour plus icon, shape or text. Text and icon contrast meets AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large text and graphics) in light and dark. Enforced by the existing contrast check.
- Serial position: in a bottom bar the most important item is first (inline-start) or last (inline-end); the Learn tab is first and the primary lesson action is last; destructive and rare items are never at either end.
- Jakob's law: use platform conventions: bottom tab bar and back swipe on phones, a left sidebar and menu-bar accelerators on desktop, bottom sheets on phones, a modal dialog on desktop, pull-down dismiss for sheets, Esc to close on desktop.
- Immediate feedback: every tap, swipe or key produces a visible change within 100 ms (pressed state, the pending state, or a toast) and, on phones where available, a light haptic for committed gestures. Pending controls ignore duplicate activation.
- Reduced motion: animation conveys state only and is replaced by instant or opacity change under reduced motion.

#### Scenario: Primary action reach
- **WHEN** the layout test renders each phone screen at 390 by 844
- **THEN** its single primary action lies in the lower 40% of the viewport, is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels and clears the safe-area inset

#### Scenario: Too many actions
- **WHEN** a view declares 2 primary actions or 3 secondary actions
- **THEN** the test fails and the additional actions move into a "more" control or a sheet

#### Scenario: Colour is not the only signal
- **WHEN** the screenshot test renders an error and a success state in greyscale
- **THEN** each is still distinguishable by icon or text

### Requirement: Phone layout rules
On widths below 48rem the client SHALL use a bottom tab bar for the four primary destinations in the existing `mobileTabs` order (Learn first), a bottom sheet in place of every centred modal (dialog on 48rem and above), and safe-area insets on every fixed edge. The bottom bar is at least 56 px high plus the inset, hides while a Lesson Slide plays in immersive mode, and never covers the primary action. Sheets have a drag handle, a snap at about 50% and full height, a scrim, a close control at inline-start of the top and the swipe-down alternative, and trap focus. A one-handed reach map governs placement: the lower third holds primary and frequent actions, the middle holds content, the upper third holds titles and rarely used controls; destructive actions are never in the lower-end corner. Landscape phones use side insets and keep the stage unobstructed.

#### Scenario: Modal becomes sheet
- **WHEN** a Learner opens the delete-note confirmation on a phone
- **THEN** it appears as a bottom sheet with the confirm control in the lower third, not as a centred modal, and as a dialog at 1280 by 800

#### Scenario: Notched device
- **WHEN** the app runs on a device with a bottom inset of 34 px
- **THEN** the tab bar content sits above the inset and no control is clipped, in portrait and in both landscape orientations

### Requirement: Design review evidence
Each per-screen pass SHALL attach English and Arabic screenshots at 390 by 844, 844 by 390 and 1280 by 800 (light and dark for the lesson player and one other screen) to `docs/design/reviews/ux-simplify-<date>/`, plus the updated copy-audit row, and SHALL pass the axe accessibility scan with no serious or critical finding.

#### Scenario: Review folder
- **WHEN** a screen pass is ready for review
- **THEN** its pull request includes the screenshots, the audit row and a passing accessibility scan

### Requirement: Simplification does not remove required disclosures
Reducing copy MUST NOT remove or shorten text required by other capabilities: privacy and consent disclosures, AI-processing notices, Credit cost and refund wording, destructive confirmations, age or moderation notices, and accessible recovery states. Such text MAY move behind a labelled link or sheet where its owning spec allows it, and MUST stay reachable in at most 2 taps.

#### Scenario: Privacy notice
- **WHEN** onboarding copy is reduced
- **THEN** the privacy notice remains reachable from the topic screen through a labelled "Privacy" control in at most 2 taps
