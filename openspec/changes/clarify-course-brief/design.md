# Design

A course brief contains topic, goal, include_topics, skip_topics, level, maths, viewing_conditions, lesson_length and language. Confirmed onboarding seeds only topic, goal, level and language; skipped setup supplies no invented context. Chat operates on request-local drafts. Each field can be edited or skipped. The model only classifies vagueness; deterministic mapping preserves learner text verbatim and prevents include/skip conflation. At most one follow-up per field is accepted by the client; the stateless engine honors is_follow_up and always advances after it.

POST /v1/course-brief/chat is bounded by 15 seconds at the API with the existing 10-second gRPC client ceiling; the configured engine per-attempt deadline is 3 seconds, at most two primary attempts and one fallback. Pricing config supplies input/output ceilings, model, attempt/rate and USD caps. Live calls stay disabled until production pricing approval and live eval promotion. Failed calls retain local answers in the equivalent form. No reservation exists to release.

POST /v1/course-brief/confirm accepts explicit consent and creates an immutable revision with UUID, SHA-256 canonical content hash and 30-day expiry. GET /v1/course-brief/revisions/{id} checks authenticated ownership and expiry. Later edits clear the client confirmation; consumers must bind the exact revision/hash. Reconfirmation creates a new revision. The E2-01 preview consumer is deferred.

Device session drafts are keyed by authenticated learner UUID and survive internal navigation, auth and shop return without URL text. They are disposable and never backed up. Confirmed revisions cascade on account deletion and are included in account exports and nightly encrypted 14-day general dumps. Expired revisions are purged nightly before database backup (within 24 hours) and on confirmation and inaccessible after 30 days; nightly backup roll-off bounds recovery retention. Restore replays existing deletion tombstones. No raw transcript is stored.

Analytics catalogue: existing ui_control_activated gains confirm_brief on course_brief; quick_reply, skip and form_fallback use the shared guided wrapper. ui_sheet_opened uses course_brief. $ai_generation reports attempt usage/provider/model/fallback only, with UUID/common properties and consent checks; no learner text. Offline fixtures cover vague/specific, skipped/empty, non-English/injection, include/skip fidelity, invalidation and failure. Live Hugging Face publication waits for owner provisioning (ADR 0011).
