Shape, depth, spacing

Radius 8 · 16 (buttons, inputs) · 24 (cards, sheets) · pill
Tactile depth: solid 4px edge in a darker shade, press moves 3px down (90ms). Cards use a thicker bottom border. No blur shadows. Spacing 4px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48. Phone gutter 20px, card padding 16px. Targets ≥ 44px; path node 72px.

Motion & sound

press 90ms · hover 140ms · sheet / progress 240ms · one completion accent 420ms (bouncy check + XP count-up). No shake on wrong answers, no confetti storm, no continuous loops outside Motion HTML slides. Reduced motion (OS or setting, stricter wins): no press travel, static check, instant sheets. Sound off by default: soft tap on select, two rising notes on completion, neutral tap on correction. Narration only after Play.
