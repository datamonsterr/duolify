duolify App design · v1 draft · 2026-11-09

Every screen, state and component for Duolify

Turn any topic into a playful, Duolingo-style learning path: short Motion HTML lessons, a lesson test you pass by resolving every idea, streaks with friends and credits for AI help. This canvas covers epics E1–E16 from docs/epics, the 24 surfaces in docs/design/screens.md, plus sharing & marketplace (E15), course steering (E16), subscription plans (E12) and the moderator console.

The learning loop

1 · TopicLearner names a topic and level.
›
2 · PreviewSample slides + sample outline. Paid, nothing created.
›
3 · OutlineConfirm cost; outline is generated and learner is enrolled.
›
4 · LessonMotion HTML slides with narration and captions.
›
5 · Lesson testEvery topic must be right. Misses return as retry variants.
›
6 · FinishXP, streak, daily goal. Next lesson is generated only now.

Theme decision

  • Duolingo-style look as requested: bright green actions, chunky 3D buttons, rounded heavy type (Nunito), zig-zag path, celebratory but short feedback.
  • Original brand: duolify wordmark and two-tile mark. No owl, mascot or Duolingo assets.
  • Overrides the ink-violet palette in docs/design/brand.md — that doc needs an update before implementation.
  • Action green is #1F7A0D so white labels pass 5.5:1; bright lime is decoration only. Tokens live in docs/design/tokens.json.
  • Desktop first: every board shows desktop frames, then mobile states. Both platforms have the same functions; desktop panels become mobile sheets.
  • Guided chat: onboarding and course creation are chats with quick replies, a live summary and an explicit confirm before anything paid.

Product rules kept

  • No hearts or lives. Mistakes queue a retry variant; no timers.
  • Streak freezes are earned by effort and applied automatically — never sold.
  • Effort never earns credits. Daily free credits (46) fund notes and tutor chat only.
  • Narration never autoplays. Captions and transcript are always available.
  • Saved lessons, notes and bookmarks stay usable at zero credits.

Open questions (from specs)

  • Prices shown are the proposed catalogue usd-launch-2026-11-08; owner adoption pending.
  • Does a personal freeze also protect a friend streak? (E6)
  • Who pays for lessons beyond a published snapshot — owner or learner? (E15, v1: learner)
  • Price of outline re-plan (proposed 55 credits, flagged). (E16)
  • Can shared narration audio be redistributed? (ADR 0009)
  • Is the clarifying chat free, and do chat edits to the sample outline cost ~5 credits? (proposed, E12)

Navigation

Phone (< 48rem)Five bottom tabs: Learn · Courses · Friends · Notes · More. More holds Leagues, Bookmarks, Credits, Profile, Settings. Header shows course switcher, streak and a labelled credit balance. Player and test use a focused shell with Exit.
Desktop (≥ 48rem, rail ≥ 75rem)224px sidebar: Learn, Courses, Marketplace, Friends, Leagues, Quick notes, Bookmarks, then Credits and Profile. 280px support rail: streak, daily goal, credits, league. See page “Desktop & moderator”.
